Straight answers

What this touches, and what it doesn’t.

You have been told by somebody that their tool touches nothing. That is never true of anything that texts your patients. Here is the honest version.

✕Never touched

  • Clinical charts, treatment history, imaging and notes: we never open those, connected or not
  • Insurance and billing records
  • Nothing is exported, resold, or used to train anything

✓Handled, under a BAA

  • Patient names and phone numbers, so messages can be sent
  • What a caller said they were calling about
  • Replies patients send back to you
  • Appointment times for anything booked through your Practera page
  • Which appointments were completed: names and numbers only, so review requests follow real visits

Why we say it that way: a text to a patient of a dental practice is protected health information, whatever anybody tells you. So there is a business associate agreement signed before anything is set up, your own login where you can see everything that goes out, and no minimum term. You can end this whenever you like. If you would rather see exactly how the data moves before you decide, ask and we will walk through it line by line.
